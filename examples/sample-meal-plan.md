## Prep ahead

- Sunday 8/23: thaw two pounds of chicken thighs in the fridge. Form the turkey meatballs and freeze them on a tray.
- Monday morning: thighs, a can of crushed tomatoes, taco spices from the pantry, and a splash of stock into the slow cooker on low.
- Wednesday before pickup: rice cooker on.
- Thursday night: pizza dough from the freezer to the fridge.
- Sunday 8/30 prep block: soup into the slow cooker first, then thaw and prep for next week.

## Leftovers night

Wednesday. Monday's taco chicken and cheese go into quesadillas with a bowl of rice.

## Notes

- Assumed a four-person household plus the cat, as listed in the profile. Pepper's food is not in this budget.
- Onions appear only in the Sunday soup, in halves that come out before serving, because of Leila's dislike. Mushrooms, olives, cilantro, and spicy seasoning are left out.
- No pork anywhere. Two meatless dinners (Thursday and Sunday). A vegetable with every dinner.
- Rough total for new purchases: about $70. That leaves roughly $90 of the $160 for breakfasts, lunches, snacks, and the olive oil and rice that are running low.
- Costs are rough estimates for a Bay Area Trader Joe's; check your receipt.
