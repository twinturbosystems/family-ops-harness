## Allergies (hard rule: never in any dish, in any form)

- Theo: peanuts and tree nuts (almonds, cashews, walnuts, pecans, pistachios, hazelnuts, pine nuts). No "may contain nuts" snacks either.
- Dana: shellfish (shrimp, crab, lobster, clams, mussels, oysters, scallops). Fin fish like salmon and cod is fine.

## Dislikes (avoid unless asked)

- Leila: mushrooms, visible onion chunks
- Theo: spicy food, dinners where everything is mixed together (he likes the parts separate)
- Sam: olives
- Dana: cilantro

## Dietary pattern

- No pork. Otherwise omnivore.
- Aim for a vegetable with every dinner and at least two meatless dinners a week.

## Time and effort

- Weeknight cooking limit: 35 minutes from start to table, Monday to Thursday.
- Friday is pizza and a movie, homemade or takeout, inside the grocery budget.
- Sunday afternoon 3 to 5 pm is batch prep time. A slow cooker or oven dinner is welcome that day.
